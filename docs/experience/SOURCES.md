# Sources & Gastronomic Research References

## 1. Culinary & Cultural Authorities
- **UNESCO Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity (2024)**: Recognition of Vietnamese Pho culture as intangible national cultural heritage.
- **Michelin Guide Vietnam (Hanoi, Da Nang, Ho Chi Minh City 2023–2024)**: Verified restaurant addresses and historical context for iconic street food stalls (e.g. Pho Thin Bo Ho, Bun Cha Huong Lien, Banh Mi Huynh Hoa, Ba Buoi Chicken Rice).
- **Vietnam National Administration of Tourism (VNAT)**: Regional culinary breakdowns across Northern (Thang Long imperial / Red River), Central (Hue Royal Court / ancient sea trade ports), and Southern (Mekong fertile waterways) culinary traditions.
- **Oxford English Dictionary**: Inclusion of "pho" (2007) and "banh mi" (2011) as recognized international English loanwords.
