# Experience Brief — Vietnamese Gastronomy & Culinary Journey

## 1. Project Scope & Purpose
- **Brand**: Viet Entry Travel (`viet-entry-travel`)
- **Experience Route**: `/culinary` (Regional Gastronomic Explorer) & `/culinary/:slug` (Deep Dish Cultural Storytelling)
- **Primary Audience**: Discerning international travelers, foodies, and cultural explorers visiting Vietnam who seek authentic regional gastronomic knowledge before booking private culinary tours.
- **Language**: English (with authentic romanized Vietnamese dish names and province accents in parentheses).
- **Primary Goals**:
  1. Transform the culinary section from a static card grid into an immersive gastronomic exploration across Vietnam's 3 major regions and 15 culinary provinces.
  2. Provide comprehensive research into culinary philosophy, indigenous herbs, terroir, and generational master techniques.
  3. Ensure every single dish features multiple verified, high-resolution, responsive photos showcasing the dish, street vendors, and ingredients.
  4. Provide frictionless calls to action: Request a Food Tour Consultation and WhatsApp direct booking (`+84338649908`).

## 2. Design Tokens & Brand System
- **Primary Brand Red**: `#b31820` (Vietnamese lacquer red, inspired by the national flag and temple pillars).
- **Secondary Accent Green**: `#4e6e1c` (Fresh young rice *cốm* & forest green, symbolizing mountain herbs and fresh produce).
- **Warm Culinary Canvas**: `#0f0f10` (Dark obsidian night for hero/lightbox) with warm paper cream `#faf8f5` for reading readability.
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: Editorial serif (`Playfair Display`, `Georgia`, serif) for cultural gravitas and sensory elegance.
  - Body & Meta: Clean humanist sans-serif (`Inter`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif) for high legibility.
- **Micro-Interactions**:
  - Parallax depth on hero banners.
  - Region & Terroir tab pills with tactile state indicators.
  - Interactive multi-photo mosaic gallery with full-screen lightbox slider.
  - Radar-style flavor profile visualization (Savory, Sweet, Sour, Spicy, Herbal Umami).
